How it works
Three steps. Zero friction.
No accounts, no emails, no nonsense.
01 — GET IN
Get your password
Your server moderator hands out the password for your server.
02 — IDENTIFY
Set up your profile
Pick a username and avatar. Set up once, remembered forever.
03 — YAP
Start chatting
12 subject channels, DMs, pins, GIFs — the full deal.
Features
Built for students, not surveillance
Everything you need to stay in sync with your class.
⚡
Real-time messaging
Firebase Firestore powers instant updates across all channels.
💬
Direct Messages
Private 1-on-1 conversations with any classmate in your section.
📚
12 Subject Channels
General, announcements, memes, and every subject your class takes.
📌
Pin, reply, edit, delete
Full message management with per-user and global delete options.
🎭
Custom avatars
Upload a custom image or animated GIF as your avatar, like Discord.
🌗
Light & dark mode
Full theme toggle that adapts every element to your preference.
